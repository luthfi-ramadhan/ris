Currently, I am an associate professor with the Department of Computer Engineering at Sriwijaya University, Indralaya, Indonesia. My research interests are in data analysis and data science. In addition to my teaching responsibilities, I actively engage in collaborative projects that explore innovative solutions for real-world problems using advanced data techniques. I also mentor students in their research endeavors by creating a hands-on learning environment that encourages critical thinking and creativity. I believe that fostering a supportive academic atmosphere is essential for nurturing the next generation of engineers and data scientists. This commitment not only enhances their educational experience but also prepares them to tackle complex challenges in the ever-evolving field of technology. By instilling a sense of curiosity and resilience, I aim to equip my students with the skills necessary to adapt and thrive in diverse situations. Ultimately, this approach empowers them to become not just proficient professionals but also thoughtful leaders in their respective fields.
